GreenJourney

Support & Privacy

GreenJourney is an iPhone app for people who collect golf courses. You tick the ones you have played, and it reads your record back to you.

Getting help

If something is broken, a course is wrong, or one is missing, please say so. Wrong architects, wrong years, wrong access types and missing courses are the most useful things you can report, because that data is the whole app.

Email

kobehaot@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug. If you are testing through TestFlight, the feedback button there sends this along automatically.

Common questions

  • Where is my record stored? On your iPhone, and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Why can't I see private clubs? They are hidden by default, since most people cannot book them. The home card says how many are hidden, with a “Show all” next to it.
  • Which states are covered? California, Massachusetts, Arizona, Colorado, Hawaii, Washington and Oregon. More are being surveyed.
  • Can I move my record to a new phone? Not yet. There is no account and no sync, so a new phone starts empty.

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

GreenJourney does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The app contains no networking code at all, so it cannot send your information anywhere even in principle.

What the app stores

Everything the app knows about you is kept locally on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage:

  • Which courses you have ticked as played
  • Any dates and course conditions you choose to record
  • Your bookmarks, and which badges you have already been shown
  • Your display preferences, such as the access filter

None of it is transmitted. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device permanently.

Sharing and comparing

The app can produce a short code representing your collection, and an image of your portrait, which you may choose to send to someone. This only ever happens when you explicitly tap share, and it goes through your own phone's share sheet to a recipient you pick. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party software development kits and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about your use is profiled, sold or shared.

What Apple sees

Two things happen outside our control, and you should know about them:

  • If you install through TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and any feedback you deliberately send, and shows them to the developer. That is Apple's system, governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • If you install from the App Store, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous, aggregated statistics such as download counts. These never identify individual people.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Should the app ever begin collecting anything, that change would be described here plainly and before it took effect.

Contact
